Build 03 — Sustainability · Dec 2025

Battery Life Cycle Assessment

SimaPro / Impact World+ / ReCiPe 2016

The engineering challenge

Evaluate disposable alkaline versus rechargeable AAA batteries on an equivalent-use basis for a bicycle rear light — accounting for production, transport, repeated use and end-of-life.

Approach

Developed the academic comparison with a functional unit of 90 hours of light per year (86.4 Wh), modelling material inventories and evaluating midpoint and endpoint environmental categories.

What the model shows

The assumed annual service compared 48 alkaline cells with a reused pair of rechargeables. Results reveal trade-offs across resource depletion, toxicity and charging energy — and depend on electricity mix and battery lifespan.

Build 04 — Machine Learning · Grenoble INP · 2026

Predictive Maintenance — Machine-Failure Classification

Supervised classification / R · caret

The engineering challenge

Predict whether a machine will fail from its sensor readings, on a dataset where only 339 of 10,000 records are failures — a 3% positive class where plain accuracy is misleading.

My contribution

I engineered and benchmarked five classifiers (logistic regression, KNN, decision tree, SVM, neural network) in R, tuned hyperparameters by cross-validation, and compared them on precision, recall and F1 rather than accuracy — handling the imbalance with resampling.

Build 06 — Machine Learning · Grenoble INP · 2026

Hiring-Callback Classification Study

Logistic regression & trees / R

The engineering challenge

Test whether an applicant's name and ethnicity change their chance of an interview callback, across 4,870 resumes with only an 8% callback rate.

My contribution

I modelled callbacks with logistic-regression and decision-tree classifiers in R, selected variables by stepwise AIC, and judged the models on ROC/AUC — separating real effects from noise under heavy class imbalance.
